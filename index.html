01 / PROFILE

Engineering better outcomes for people & planet.

Core Philosophy

"Water infrastructure isn't just about pumps and piping; it is the fundamental circulatory system of modern society."

From a treatment plant's first process design to the moment it starts serving a community, I bring clarity to complex projects and accountability to every outcome.

My work sits at the intersection of engineering, leadership, and long-term value. I build teams that deliver, systems that last, and partnerships that make ambitious infrastructure possible.

Over two and a half decades, I have spearheaded mega-turnkey water installations across the Persian Gulf, Maldives atolls, and heavy industrial petrochemical corridors in India. Whether orchestrating seawater desalination in high-salinity marine basins or recovering pure condensate through Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD), my focus is absolute operational reliability and environmental stewardship.
